What this is

We opened Frameo, tapped Upgrade, and read your paywall against the report.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion across the market, not inside one app. We pointed that at Frameo: opened the app, went to the Menu, tapped Upgrade, tried both plans and both billing periods, and closed it. Then we read your App Store listing and held the screen up against State of Paywalls 2026.

What follows is four ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each is built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours. Benchmarks are for Photo & Video apps across all regions.

We did not redesign your app

The four ideas

Ranked by what we would test first.

Offer changes before cosmetics. Each one links straight to the screen in the prototype.

01

Explain the 14 days before they start

Your trial is twice as long as most, but the paywall never says what happens on day 14 or what it costs. 16,99 € first appears in Apple’s sheet, after the tap. A three-step line above the button fixes both.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026 (Photo & Video): 54.6% of trials are cancelled within 24 hours. ARPU +12–36%Observed in winning
A/B tests
02

Give a second chance when they close

Today the X drops people back on the Menu and the paywall never comes back. One sheet with Frameo+ alone, shown as 1,42 € a month (your 16,99 € a year), catches people who closed because two tiers and four prices felt like a lot.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026: only 4% of apps show a paywall after close; it converts at 0.88× the main one. ARPU +10–15%Based on
Adapty data
03

Put your 4.9 stars on the paywall

4.9 from 449K ratings and 30 million downloads sit on your App Store page, not on the screen where people decide. One line under the headline brings them there.

📊 State of Paywalls 2026: only 27% of top paywalls show social proof. CR +10–30%Observed in winning
A/B tests
04

Speak to the person who tapped Upgrade

The paywall opens from the Menu, right under the person’s own photo and name. Then it says “Get more out of Frameo” to everyone. Opening on the name you already have is a cheap copy test.
